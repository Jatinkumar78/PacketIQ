# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in PacketIQ, whether by filing an issue, reviewing a
patch, reporting a vulnerability or asking a question, is entitled to a
harassment-free experience. We pledge to act in ways that keep this project open
and welcoming regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

* Be direct about the work and considerate about the person. Review the patch,
  not the author.
* Accept that a technical disagreement is normal and that evidence settles it.
  This project asks contributors to prove claims with measurements, so "show me
  the measurement" is a fair request, not an insult.
* Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until the
  evidence says otherwise.
* Give credit for work you build on, and say plainly when you are unsure.

## What is not acceptable

* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention of any kind.
* Insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Harassment in public or in private, including sustained disruption of
  discussion.
* Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical address, an
  email address or an employer, without their explicit permission.
* Using this project to attack systems you are not authorised to test, or
  requesting help to do so. PacketIQ is a defensive tool and is maintained as
  one. Requests for offensive tooling, evasion of detection, or analysis aimed
  at a third party's network without authorisation are out of scope and will be
  closed.
* Submitting fabricated results. Inventing a measurement, a detection figure, a
  threat-intelligence indicator or a benchmark number misleads every person who
  later relies on it, and in a forensics tool that can affect a real
  investigation. It is treated as a conduct matter, not only a technical one.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, which includes issues, pull requests,
commit messages, code review, discussions, and the project's documentation. It
also applies when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that breaks these rules, report it
privately to the maintainer. Do not open a public issue.

* Use the repository's private reporting form at
  [Security > Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Jatinkumar78/PacketIQ/security/advisories/new).
  It is the project's only private channel today, and although it is labelled
  for vulnerabilities it reaches the maintainer and nobody else. Start the
  report with "Code of Conduct" so it is triaged correctly.
* Or contact the maintainer through the profile at
  [github.com/Jatinkumar78](https://github.com/Jatinkumar78).

Include what happened, where, and roughly when. Screenshots or links help. You
will get an acknowledgement within a few days.

Reports are handled in confidence. The reporter's identity is not disclosed to
the person reported about, or to anyone else, without the reporter's consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will explain the reason for any moderation decision when asked. Responses are
proportionate and escalate only as far as needed:

| Response | When it is used |
|---|---|
| Private correction | A single inappropriate comment or an unprofessional remark. A private note explains what was wrong and why. |
| Warning | A pattern of behaviour, or a single serious incident. Continued interaction with the people involved is not permitted for a stated period. |
| Temporary ban | A sustained pattern, or harassment of an individual. No public or private interaction with the project for a stated period. |
| Permanent ban | A repeated pattern after a ban, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward a group. |

The maintainer has the right to remove, edit or reject any contribution that
breaks these rules, and to moderate the project's spaces accordingly.

## Attribution

These standards follow the spirit and structure of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, and
are written in this project's own words rather than copied, with two additions
specific to PacketIQ: the clause on authorised, defensive use, and the clause on
fabricated results.
