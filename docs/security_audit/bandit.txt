On the 14 suppressions
----------------------
"Skipped due to specifically being disabled" counts lines carrying a targeted
`# nosec BXXX` with a written justification. They are targeted by rule ID, never
a blanket `# nosec`, and "Total lines skipped (#nosec): 0" above confirms no line
is exempted from scanning wholesale. They sit on 12 lines (two lines carry two
rule IDs each, hence 14), and each is a case where the pattern bandit flags is
the correct code:

  - B404 ×2, B603 ×4, B607 ×2 (subprocess) — net_interfaces.py and
    capture_setup.py. Every call is list-form with a fixed argv and no shell;
    where a user-derived value is involved at all ($USER in the capture-privilege
    setup) it is validated against a strict charset before use.
  - B112 ×4 (try/except/continue) — webapp/app.py, enrichment/update.py,
    export/pcap_slicer.py, detection/yara_scan.py. Each skips one malformed
    item (an unreadable capture, a bad feed line, a corrupt packet, a broken YARA
    rule) and carries on with the rest, which is the intended behaviour.
  - B104 ×2 (hardcoded bind-all address) — utils/helpers.py and
    net_interfaces.py. Both are the literal "0.0.0.0" used as a sentinel to
    compare against and filter out, never as a bind target.

Because the scan is clean at every severity, the CI step is blocking: a new
finding is a genuine regression rather than pre-existing debt, and it fails the
build instead of scrolling past in a green log.
